Festival venue: Wisdom Valley

Wisdom Valley
Khao Mai Kaew, Bang Lamung District, Chonburi 20150, Thailand

An open-air festival venue among hills, water and palm trees, about 30 minutes from central Pattaya. Six festival stages.

Festival age limit (20+)

Strictly 20+. You must turn 20 on or before 11 December 2026. Anyone younger is refused entry, even with an adult.

Carry your passport or a government photo ID.

Entry requirements & festival bracelet

  • Your festival bracelet is your ticket. It is posted to the main buyer before the event.
  • The bracelet is also used to pay inside the festival, which is cashless.
  • Bring your bracelet, booking confirmation and ID.

Festival re-entry policy

  • No same-day re-entry once you leave the grounds.
  • Full Madness and Full Madness Comfort passes: come back the next day.
  • Day and Day Comfort passes: no re-entry.

How to get to Wisdom Valley

  • Fly into Bangkok Suvarnabhumi (BKK), about 90 minutes by road, or U-Tapao (UTP), the closest airport.
  • From BKK, buses to Pattaya leave from Level 1, Gate 8. Take a taxi or Grab for the last stretch to Wisdom Valley.
  • Allow extra time around opening and closing. Festival traffic is heavy.

Where to stay in Pattaya

  • There is no camping on site. Stay in Pattaya or nearby.
  • North Pattaya and Ban Chang are closest to the venue.
  • December is peak season, so book early and arrive a day before the festival.

Line-up, stage times and any rule changes are published by the organiser in the official festival app. *Details may change; reconfirm before travel.

Festival event details

Music festival dates: Friday 11 to Sunday 13 December 2026 at Wisdom Valley, Khao Mai Kaew, near Pattaya, Thailand. Festival gates open at 1 PM each day. Strictly 20+ with a passport or photo ID, and the festival bracelet is required for entry.
